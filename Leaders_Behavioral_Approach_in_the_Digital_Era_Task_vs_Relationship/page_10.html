LEADERSHIP STYLES ACROSS TUCKMAN TEAM STAGES
Mapping Grid Positions to Team Development | Farhan et al. (2024)
Early Team Development
Stage 1: Forming → Directive (5–7, 5–7)
Defines tasks and roles clearly; essential when trust is low and structure is needed for clarity.
Stage 2: Storming → Pacesetting (7–9, 5–7)
Sets high standards and drives toward goals while managing conflict and creating healthy competition.
Mature Team Development
Stage 3: Norming → Servant (5–7, 7–9)
Empowers self-directed teams; builds trust through coaching and reduced control.
Stage 4: Performing → Democratic (7–9, 7–9)
Full Team Management style; encourages collaborative decisions and high member independence.
THE STRATEGIC SHIFT: Digital leaders must not maintain a fixed style—the (9,9) Team Management style is the destination, not the starting point.
