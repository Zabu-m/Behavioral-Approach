Purpose and Context of Digital Transformation
Examining how the COVID-19 pandemic forced a shift to remote work in the UAE, making effective digital leadership and behavioral adaptation an urgent priority.
70%
Of the UAE workforce worked remotely for the first time during the pandemic.
Aspiring to be a global hub for intelligent systems and digitalization.
The Behavioral Challenge
  • Communicate direction and vision across digital platforms without physical proximity.
  • Maintain psychological connection and motivation for remote employees.
  • Success: Increases morale, self-efficacy, and work performance.
  • Failure: Leads to loss of control over operations and personnel.
Research Objectives
Determine the behavioral leadership orientation (Task vs. Relationship) of UAE leaders managing digitally.
Analyze if individual (age, gender, nationality) or organizational differences influence digital leadership styles.
