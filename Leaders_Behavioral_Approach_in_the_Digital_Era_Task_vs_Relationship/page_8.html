GENERATIONAL DIFFERENCES MILLENNIALS VS GENERATION X
UAE Digital Transformation Context | Farhan et al. (2024)
Millennials (30–39 yrs)
Primary: Task Orientation
  • Significantly higher task focus (Mean Rank: 263.50).
  • Digitally native and tech-savvy orientation.
  • Efficient at managing remote workflows through structured digital tools.
* 20–29 years group serves as the study baseline.
Gen-X (40+ yrs)
Primary: Relationship Orientation
  • Significantly higher relationship focus (Mean Rank: 259.94).
  • Prioritize personal bonds and psychological safety.
  • Leverage relational behaviors to sustain performance in remote environments.
Strategic Insight: Seniority is not the sole criterion for digital success. Organizations benefit from the task fluency of Millennials and the relational depth of Gen-X to achieve full behavioral integration.
