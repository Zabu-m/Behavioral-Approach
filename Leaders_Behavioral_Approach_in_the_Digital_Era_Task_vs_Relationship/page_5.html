RESEARCH METHODOLOGY AND SAMPLE PROFILE
UAE Digital Transformation Context | Farhan et al. (2024)
Research Instrument (LBDQ)
20-Item Questionnaire: 10 items measuring Task Orientation and 10 items for Relationship Orientation.
Psychometric Rigor: Composite reliability exceeded 0.94, confirming high validity and internal consistency.
5-Point Likert Scale: Ranging from 1 (Never) to 5 (Always) to capture behavioral nuances.
Analysis & Sampling
Sample Group: 476 respondents from public and private sectors in Abu Dhabi & Dubai.
Collection Method: Snowball sampling via professional networks (LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Email).
Tools: IBM SPSS for descriptive analysis and SmartPLS 3 for Structural Equation Modelling.
Sample Demographics
Attribute Key Findings
Age Group 20–39 Years: 96% (Millennials & Gen-Z dominant)
Gender Female: 59.5% · Male: 40.3%
Nationality UAE Citizens: 71.5% · Residents: 28.5%
Education University Graduates: 73.5% (Inc. 12% Post-Grad)
Sector Public Sector: 66% · Private/Semi: 34%
* Data collected over 3 weeks via online survey platform (MonkeySurvey).
