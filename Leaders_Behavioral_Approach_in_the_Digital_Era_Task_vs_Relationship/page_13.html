Key Takeaways and Conclusion
UAE Digital Transformation Context | Farhan et al. (2024)
Team Management Dominance
  • 90% of UAE leaders score high on both task and relationship orientations.
  • The (9,9) style is the optimal baseline for digital leadership success.
Human Connection Mechanism
  • Psychological safety is the mechanism that sustains task performance.
  • Top behaviors: comfort, friendliness, fairness, and active communication.
Generational Synergy
  • Millennials lean task-oriented; Gen-X lean relationship-oriented.
  • Organizations benefit from developing both dimensions across all ages.
Adaptive Framework
  • Leadership must evolve: Directive → Pacesetting → Servant → Democratic.
  • Styles must map to the team's maturity and development stages.
"Behavior integration is the key to digital viability."
Prioritizing people drives sustainable production.
Digital leadership is a behavioural challenge, not just a technological one: Structure without connection becomes control; connection without structure becomes drift.
