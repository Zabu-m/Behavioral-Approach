THE CORE TENSION: STRUCTURE VS CONNECTION
UAE Digital Transformation Context | Farhan et al. (2024)
The Common Misconception

The belief that remote work requires increased task monitoring and tighter control mechanisms to preserve organizational productivity.

Outcome: Risk of Micromanagement
The Data Reality (UAE)

UAE leaders prioritized psychological safety over control. Relationship behaviors outscored task behaviors in digital settings.

  • Comfort & Psychological Safety (Mean: 4.212)
  • Problem Solving & Quality Focus (Mean: 4.102)
The Integration Imperative

Task and relationship are not tradeoffs; relationship behaviors are the mechanism through which task performance is sustained.
