THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK: BLAKE AND MOUTON LEADERSHIP GRID
UAE Digital Transformation Context | Farhan et al. (2024)
Concern for Production (Tasks)
Represents the horizontal axis (X). Focuses on organizational efficiency, goal achievement, and high-quality task execution.
Concern for People (Relationships)
Represents the vertical axis (Y). Focuses on employee needs, psychological safety, social support, and professional development.
The Scoring System (1-9)
  • Scale: Leaders are rated from 1 (Low) to 9 (High) on each dimension.
  • Integration: The grid creates a coordinate system (X, Y) to identify behavioral patterns.
  • Outcome: Produces five primary leadership styles based on the relative balance of task and people focus.
  • Application: Used in this study to evaluate leadership during digital/remote shifts.
Source: Adapted from Blake and Mouton (1964) - The Leadership Grid Framework
