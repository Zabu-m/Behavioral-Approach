PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS FOR LEADERS AND ORGANIZATIONS
Recommendations based on UAE Digital Leadership Empirical Research | Farhan et al. (2024)
STRATEGIES FOR LEADERS
Adopt Team Management (9,9) Baseline Maintain high concern for both task performance and interpersonal relationships to foster engagement in remote settings.
Adaptive Style to Team Stages Transition from Directive (Forming) to Democratic (Performing) as the digital team matures and builds trust.
Prioritize Psychological Safety Actively build comfort, fairness, and open communication to mitigate risks of isolation in virtual environments.
Focus on Quality Work Signals Encourage high-quality standards rather than micromanagement; task focus should signal support, not control.
STRATEGIES FOR ORGANIZATIONS
Capability Over Seniority Promote based on digital fluency and behavioral orientation. Millennials show strong task-leadership capabilities in digital eras.
Invest in Behavioral Training Technical competence alone is insufficient; training should focus on the integration of task and relationship behaviors.
Digital as a Behavioral Challenge Frame digital transformation as a shift in human interaction patterns, not just a deployment of technological tools.
Test Classic Models Digitally Utilize established frameworks like the Leadership Grid to audit and develop digital leadership talent.
