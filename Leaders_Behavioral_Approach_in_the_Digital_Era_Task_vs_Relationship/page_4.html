The Five Major Leadership Styles
Blake & Mouton Leadership Grid | Behavioral Framework
Leadership Style Grid Position Behavioral Focus & Characteristics
Authority-Compliance (9, 1) High Task / Low People: Efficiency results from arranging work conditions so human elements interfere to a minimum. Controlling and demanding.
Country-Club Management (1, 9) Low Task / High People: Primary focus on satisfying needs of people for relationships. Leads to a comfortable, friendly atmosphere but weak results.
Impoverished Management (1, 1) Low Task / Low People: Exertion of minimum effort to get required work done. Leaders are disinterested, detached, and essentially apathetic.
Middle-of-the-Road (5, 5) Moderate Task / Moderate People: Adequate performance is possible through balancing the necessity to get work out with maintaining morale.
Team Management Research Goal (9, 9) High Task / High People: Work accomplishment is from committed people; interdependence through a "common stake" in organizational purpose.
